Personal Profile

Elegant lingerie design with a precise commercial eye.

Claire Dupont is a 39-year-old lingerie designer and business owner based in Brooklyn. Her work focuses on refined fit, polished styling, and pieces that help women feel confident, comfortable, and beautifully composed.

With years of experience in women's intimate apparel, Claire brings together fabric knowledge, boutique merchandising, and a modern European-American sense of elegance.

The Atelier

Design work shaped by texture, proportion, and quiet luxury.

From lace selection and silhouette planning to in-store styling, Claire's boutique is built around intimate apparel that feels elevated without losing comfort.
